✅ Entry Checklist

  • 1.Market Structure: Price must be in a confirmed uptrend — higher highs and higher lows on the daily chart.
  • 2.Above Key EMAs: Price must be above EMA 10, 20, and 50 at entry. EMA 50 must be above EMA 200.
  • 3.EMA Compression: EMAs 10/20/50 must have recently converged (spread < 3% of EMA50).
  • 4.Breakout Candle: Entry on breakout candle above the EMA cluster — ideally with above-average volume.
  • 5.Not Extended: Price must not be more than 8–10% above EMA50 at entry. Avoid chasing.
  • 6.Risk Defined: Stop loss must be calculated BEFORE entry. Know exact risk ₹/$ amount.
  • 7.R:R Minimum: Reward-to-risk must be at least 2:1 to T1 target before taking the trade.

🚫 Never Enter When

  • ✗Price is below EMA 50 or EMA 50 is sloping downward.
  • ✗The broader market index (Nifty / S&P 500) is in a downtrend — over 5% below its 50-day EMA.
  • ✗Major earnings announcement within 5 trading days.
  • ✗Stock is more than 10% extended above EMA50 (late entry).
  • ✗You are chasing a stock that already ran 5%+ on a breakout day.
  • ✗Risk amount for the trade would exceed 2% of total portfolio.
  • ✗You already have 6+ open positions — wait for an exit before adding.

📍 Stop Loss Placement

EMA Bounce Entry

Stop below the low of the breakout candle OR below EMA20 — whichever is lower. Typically 1.5–3% below entry.

Base Breakout Entry

Stop below the base/consolidation low. Give 1–2% buffer below the prior swing low to avoid shakeouts.

Trailing Stop

Once price reaches T1, trail stop to breakeven. Once above T1 by 5%, trail stop to below EMA10 on daily.

⚙️ Scaling Out — Partial Exits

At Target 1 (T1)

Sell 33–50% of position. Move stop to breakeven on remaining shares. Lock in profit, remove risk.

At Target 2 (T2)

Sell another 33% of original position. Trail remaining stop to below EMA10 on daily chart.

Let Winners Run

Keep final 17–33% as a "free runner". Only exit if price closes below EMA20 on daily OR a major pattern breaks.

➕ Adding to Winners

Only add to a position when ALL conditions are met:

  • ✓ Original position is profitable (above entry by >3%).
  • ✓ Stock forms a new tight base or EMA pullback setup.
  • ✓ Market conditions remain healthy (index above EMA50).
  • ✓ Total risk on position (including add) stays under 2% portfolio.
  • ✓ Add size is 50% or less of original position size.

⚠️ Never Average Down

Do NOT add to a losing position. If a trade goes against you, respect your stop and exit. Adding down increases risk and ties up capital in a broken thesis.

  • ☐ Adjust trailing stops if needed
  • ☐ Scan for new setups for next session
  • ☐ Review any trades closed today

🛑 Mandatory Exit Signals

  • 1.Stop Hit: Price closes below your stop loss — exit IMMEDIATELY, no hesitation, no hoping.
  • 2.EMA 50 Break: Price closes below EMA50 on the daily chart after a sustained uptrend.
  • 3.Earnings Gap Down: Stock gaps down >5% on earnings — exit at open or on first available price.
  • 4.Market Crash: Index drops >3% in a day — reduce all positions by 50% immediately.
  • 5.Thesis Broken: The reason you bought no longer holds — exit regardless of price level.

🎯 Target-Based Exits

T1

Target 1 — 2R

Sell 33–50% of shares. Typically 1.5–3× the initial risk distance above entry.

T2

Target 2 — 4R

Sell another 33%. Typically 3–5× the initial risk distance. Trail remainder below EMA10.

TT

Trailing Target — Let run

Final tranche: exit when price closes below EMA10 or a 10% pullback from highs — whichever first.

🧠 Psychological Exit Rules

  • → If you are considering "moving your stop down just a little" — don't. Exit instead.
  • → A 1R loss is fine. A 3R loss from letting it run means you broke the rules.
  • → Don't exit profitable trades because they "look extended". Let price tell you when.
  • → Taking a 0.5R early profit is almost always worse than staying to T1.
  • → When in doubt, exit half — it removes the emotional pressure and keeps upside.
  • → Write your exit plan BEFORE you enter the trade. Stick to it under all emotions.

📐 Position Sizing Formula

Risk $ = Portfolio × Risk%

Risk per share = Entry − Stop

Shares = Risk $ ÷ Risk/share

Example: ₹5,00,000 × 1% = ₹5,000 risk

Entry ₹250, Stop ₹235 → risk/share = ₹15

Shares = 5000 ÷ 15 = 333 shares

🎯 EMA Squeeze Pattern

  1. 1. Price above EMA 50 & 200. EMA50 > EMA200.
  2. 2. EMA 10, 20, 50 converge — spread <3% of EMA50.
  3. 3. Price coils in tight range (low volatility).
  4. 4. Breakout candle above EMA cluster on volume.
  5. 5. Entry on breakout bar close or next open.
  6. 6. Stop below EMA20 / below breakout low.

🔑 Golden Rules

  • 1. Never risk more than 1–2% of portfolio on one trade.
  • 2. Always define your stop BEFORE entering.
  • 3. Let winners run; cut losers short and fast.
  • 4. 3 losses in a row → take a day off, review.
  • 5. Never move stops against you. Only trail in your favour.
  • 6. Track every trade — no tracking = no improvement.

📊 Market Conditions

Trending up: Index above rising EMA 50. Be aggressive — full position sizes.
Choppy: Index between EMA 20–50. Reduce size by 50%, tighter stops.
Downtrend: Index below falling EMA 50. Cash is a position. No new longs.

⚡ Quick Maths

Risk% = (Entry − Stop) / Entry × 100

e.g. (250−235)/250 = 6%

R:R = (Target − Entry) / (Entry − Stop)

e.g. (280−250)/(250−235) = 2:1

Breakeven Win Rate = 1 / (1 + R:R)

2:1 trade needs 33% win rate to break even

Swing Trading Playbook
Daily reference · EMA squeeze breakout system
Risk first. Always.
📈 Entry Checklist
  • Uptrend: Price above EMA 10, 20, 50 & 200. EMA50 above EMA200.
  • EMA Squeeze: EMA 10/20/50 spread < 3% of EMA50 — compression confirmed.
  • Breakout candle: Price breaks above EMA cluster on above-average volume.
  • Not extended: Price within 8% of EMA50. No chasing.
  • R:R ≥ 2:1 to T1 before entering. No exceptions.
  • Market healthy: Index (Nifty / S&P) above its 50-day EMA.
  • Stop defined: Know exact loss in ₹/$ before placing the order.
🚫 Never Enter When
  • Price is below EMA50 or EMA50 is sloping down.
  • Broader market index is > 5% below its 50-day EMA.
  • Earnings within 5 trading days.
  • Stock already ran 5%+ on breakout day (chasing).
  • Risk on this trade exceeds 2% of portfolio.
  • You already have 6 or more open positions.
  • You are revenge-trading after a recent loss.
🔵 EMA Squeeze Pattern
1Price in uptrend: above EMA50 & EMA200. EMA50 > EMA200.
2EMA 10, 20, 50 converge tightly — spread < 3% of EMA50.
3Price coils in a tight range — volatility contracts.
4Breakout candle closes above EMA cluster with volume expansion.
5Enter on breakout bar close or next open — not late.
6Stop below EMA20 or below breakout candle low — whichever is lower.
🧮 Position Sizing
Risk ₹/$ = Portfolio × Risk%
Risk/share = Entry − Stop
Shares = Risk ₹/$ ÷ Risk/share
─────────────────────────────
₹5,00,000 × 1% = ₹5,000 risk
Entry ₹250 − Stop ₹235 = ₹15/share
Shares = 5,000 ÷ 15 = 333 shares
  • Never risk more than 1–2% per trade.
  • Never add to a losing position. Cut it.
  • Add to winners only after original is in profit > 3%.

🛑 Mandatory Exit Signals
  • Stop hit: Exit immediately. No hoping. No moving stops down.
  • EMA50 daily close break: Exit remaining position.
  • Earnings gap-down > 5%: Exit at open price.
  • Market crash > 3% in a day: Cut all positions 50%.
  • Thesis broken: The reason you bought no longer holds — exit now.

Full position sizes. Be aggressive on setups.

Reduce size 50%. Tighten stops. Be selective.

🔑 The 7 Golden Rules
  1. Never risk more than 1–2% of portfolio on any single trade.
  2. Define your stop loss before entering — not after.
  3. Cut losses fast. Let winners run to T1, T2, then trail.
  4. Never move a stop against you. Only trail in your favour.
  5. After 3 consecutive losses — stop trading, review your process.
  6. Never average down into a losing position. Cut it instead.
  7. Track every trade. No tracking equals no improvement.
